About

I'm a UX researcher with an MSc in Human-Computer Interaction from the University of Leicester. I've researched AI products, consumer apps and services for startups and clients in Canada, the US, the UK and beyond.

My favourite moment in any project is the one sentence from a user that changes what the team builds. Most recently, I co-founded SnapQuote, where I led research and design for an AI quoting app that won the ProductBC Build-a-thon.

Methods: interviews, usability testing, surveys, heuristic evaluation, A/B testing, ethnographic observation, card sorting, personas and journey maps.
Tools: Qualtrics, SurveyMonkey, UserTesting, UserZoom, Maze, Dovetail, Smartlook, Mixpanel, Google Analytics, SPSS, Excel, Power BI, Figma.
