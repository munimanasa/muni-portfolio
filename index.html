About me

A researcher who turns what people say into what teams build.

I've worked with AI startups, consumer apps, freelance clients and a university library, across AI products, dating and social apps, e-commerce, banking, tourism and public services.

How I work

The right method for each stage of a product

I choose methods by the question the team needs answered at each stage.

  1. 1

    Discover

    What problem do people actually have?

    • Interviews in context
    • Stakeholder interviews
    • Field observation
    See it in: Leicester Library →
  2. 2

    Explore

    What should we build, and for whom?

    • Competitive analysis
    • Personas and journey maps
    • Card sorting
    See it in: SnapQuote →
  3. 3

    Test

    Does it work, and does it fit the need?

    • Moderated usability testing
    • SUS and task ratings
    • AI evaluation
    See it in: Skimli AI →
  4. 4

    Listen

    What happens once it's live?

    • Session recordings and heatmaps
    • Analytics and funnels
    • Surveys
    See it in: BeTickled →

My rule at every stage: keep evidence and guesses apart, and end with a decision. If a finding doesn't change something, I haven't finished.
